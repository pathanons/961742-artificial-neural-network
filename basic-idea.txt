We would like to make anormaly detection of stock price focusing on:
- Black swan
- Herding 
in U.S. Stock price

We will separate our area of interested into three main parts;
1. Dimensional Reduction; 
    using any deep learning to reduce the high frquency domain in to some feature space 
    and use this to compare between normal and anormaly.
2. Learning Representation;
    using an idea of autoencoder to encoder stock price signal into some latent space and then use the decoder 
    to map back into the signal space then we will use this latent space with unsupervised learning process to separate between normal annd abnormal.
3. Building End-to-End for detect anormaly signal in the stock price.
